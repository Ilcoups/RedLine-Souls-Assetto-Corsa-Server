## FAQ for Players

**Q: How do I teleport?**
A: Press T, type `/teleport` or `/tp`, browse and click!

**Q: Do I need to download anything?**
A: Nope! If you have CSP 0.2.0+, it works automatically.

**Q: Where can I teleport?**
A: 157 locations including Daikoku PA, Shibuya, all C1 sections, Route B, Route K1, and more!

**Q: Can I teleport with friends?**
A: Yes! Everyone can use it. Perfect for meet-ups!

**Q: Does it cost anything?**
A: Free! It's a server feature.

**Q: What's the point number for Daikoku?**
A: POINT_65 - just search "Daikoku" in the teleport menu!

**Q: Can I teleport during a session?**
A: Yes, anytime! Great for repositioning or exploring.

**Q: Will this lag the server?**
A: No performance impact - CSP handles it client-side.
