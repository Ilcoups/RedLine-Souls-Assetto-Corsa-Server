# JDM GRIND MESSAGES - Shutoko Edition

## Philosophy
- **Short**: 2-3 words max (you're at 200+ kph, not reading essays)
- **Punchy**: Hit different, no generic bullshit
- **JDM Culture**: References the grind, the lifestyle, the vibe
- **No Cringe**: Sarcastic but motivating

```yaml
CollisionMessages:
  - "Wall tap L"
  - "300HP to guardrail"
  - "Insurance claim time"
  - "Bodykit RIP"
  - "Paint penalty"
  - "Ramen fund hit"
  - "Rails don't move"
  - "GG widebody"
  - "Aero delete"
  - "Skill issue"
```

## Context
These fire when:
- **Collision**: You hit ANYTHING (walls, cars, barriers)
- **Overtake**: Clean pass, 7m+ gap
- **Close Overtake**: Sketchy pass, 4-7m gap (the spicy ones)

Messages flash briefly on screen - you're mid-drift at 180kph grinding points on C1, not stopping to read paragraphs.
