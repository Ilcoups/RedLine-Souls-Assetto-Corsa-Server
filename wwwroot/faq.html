Everything you need to know about RedLine Souls

What is RedLine Souls?

RedLine Souls is a free Assetto Corsa traffic server featuring the Shutoko Revival Project (Tokyo Metropolitan Expressway). We host 217 active monthly players who race high-performance cars in realistic traffic simulations on Tokyo's iconic highway network.

Think of it as virtual street racing on Tokyo's famous midnight highways - the same roads where legendary racing crews made history.

How do I join RedLine Souls server?

Two easy ways:

  1. Quick Join: Click the "Join Server" button on our homepage
  2. Manual Join: Open Assetto Corsa Content Manager → Online → Server Browser → Search "RedLine Souls" or use IP: 188.245.183.146:8081

Content Manager will automatically download all required mods on your first join (takes 5-10 minutes).

What track does RedLine Souls use?

We run on the Shutoko Revival Project (SRP) - a highly detailed recreation of Tokyo's Metropolitan Expressway system.

Famous sections include:

  • 🌃 C1 Inner Loop
  • 🌃 Wangan/Bayshore Route
  • 🌃 Yokohane Line
  • 🌃 Rainbow Bridge

This is where real Japanese street racing culture was born in the 1970s-90s.

What are the server rules?

RedLine Souls promotes respectful racing:

  1. 🚫 No ramming or intentional crashing
  2. 🤝 Respect traffic flow and other racers
  3. 💡 Use turn signals when changing lanes (lol yeah right, nobody does this)
  4. 💬 Keep Discord communication friendly
  5. 🏎️ Drive like you would in real life (but faster!)

We're here to enjoy JDM culture and highway racing together.
